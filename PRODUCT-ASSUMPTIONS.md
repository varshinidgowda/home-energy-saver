 Product Assumptions: Home Energy Saver

Inspired by: Energy Consumption Tracker (Product Hunt)
Our difference: a simplified version for students and families, with
saving tips and dummy data.

 1. What product are you planning to build?
A web app to track home appliance electricity usage.

 2. What problem does it solve?
People don't know which appliances raise their electricity bill.

 3. Who are the target users?
Students in hostels and families managing household bills.

 4. What is the main use case?
Add appliances and daily hours, then see monthly units and cost.

 5. What are the core features?
Dashboard, add appliance, usage chart, saving tips.

 6. What assumptions are you making about the users?
Users know their appliance watts and daily usage hours.

 7. What will be included in your MVP?
4 pages, reusable components, React Context, dummy data.

 8. What will NOT be included in your MVP?
Login, backend, smart meter connection, payments.
